LKCMedicine Voice Studio · Frequently asked questions

Your voice for LKCMedicine teaching

Voice Studio creates a synthetic copy of your voice (a "voice model") from recordings you provide. Please read this carefully. You choose whether to take part, and you can change your mind at any time.

About Voice Studio

What is Voice Studio?

An LKCMedicine service that turns about 30 minutes of your recorded speech into a private synthetic copy of your voice, which can then narrate teaching materials or answer students in LKCMedicine teaching tools.

Who can use it?

Anyone taking part in LKCMedicine teaching. Sign in with any Microsoft account: your NTU or other work/school account, or a personal Microsoft account (Outlook, Hotmail).

I only have Gmail. Can I sign in?

Yes. On the Microsoft sign-in page choose "Create one" and enter your Gmail address. Microsoft emails you a code and your Gmail becomes a Microsoft sign-in. You only do this once.

Do I have to take part?

No. It is entirely voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time.

Consent and privacy

What am I agreeing to?

That we may train a voice model from recordings of your own voice and use it only for teaching and learning in LKCMedicine courses and programmes. You accept the consent form once, before your first recording; the version you accepted is stored with your recordings.

Where can my voice be used?

Only in LKCMedicine (NTU) courses and programmes — never for advertising, never outside LKCMedicine teaching, and never sold or shared with third parties for their own use.

Who can hear or use my voice?

Only you, plus a small team of LKCMedicine Digital Learning super users who train it, check its quality and provide support. No other lecturer or student can select it in Voice Studio.

Where is my data kept?

In LKCMedicine-controlled cloud storage in Singapore; the GPU that trains and runs voices is in Seoul (South Korea).

How do I withdraw or delete my voice?

Contact the LKCMedicine Digital Learning team. We delete your recordings and voice models and stop using your voice in new material; anything already published is removed or replaced on request.

Will students know it is AI?

Yes. Speech generated by these tools is AI-generated, and LKCMedicine teaching tools identify it as such.

Recording

How much audio do I need?

About 30 minutes of clear speech is ideal; 10 minutes is the minimum for a usable voice. You can split it across as many files and sessions as you like.

Do I have to read the script?

No. The guided script makes it easy and covers different moods (warm, serious, curious, calm…), but your own lecture recordings work too. Mixing both is great.

Why does the script have mood cues?

A voice trained only on flat reading can only sound flat. Reading some sections warmly, some seriously and some as questions teaches the model to sound natural in more situations.

Any recording tips?

Use a quiet room without echo, keep about a hand-span from the microphone, keep that distance steady, and pause briefly between sentences. If you stumble, pause and re-read the sentence.

Which files can I upload?

WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC or OGG. Recordings made in the browser are saved as WAV automatically.

Training, quality check and testing

How long does training take?

About 30 minutes per voice. Voices train one at a time in a fair queue; you see an estimated finish time when you submit and we email it to you.

What happens after training?

A super user listens to your voice. If it is good, we email you a reserved time to test it. If not, we retrain it with adjusted settings, or ask you to record again.

Why do I get a time slot?

Voices run on one shared GPU, which can serve one voice at a time. During your slot it runs only your voice, so it is fast and private.

How do I test my voice?

Open the link in your email (or sign in to Voice Studio) during your slot. You can chat with your voice, type text for it to read aloud, and correct how it pronounces words.

It says a word wrongly — can I fix it?

Yes. In the Pronunciation panel, add the word and how it should sound (for example, "koh hee sin"), or give exact sounds in ARPAbet. Corrections apply only to your voice.

How do I give feedback?

Use the star rating and comment box on the test page. The Voice Studio team reads every comment.

Full consent form (version 2026-11-20)

What we are asking you to do

  • Record yourself reading our guided script, upload recordings you already have (for example, past lectures), or both. About 30 minutes of clear speech works best.
  • Only submit recordings of your own voice. Do not include other people speaking, such as students asking questions or a co-presenter.
  • Remove anything confidential from uploaded recordings first, such as patient details or student names.

What we collect

  • The audio you record or upload, and the voice model trained from it.
  • Your sign-in email and display name, so the voice is linked to you and only you can test it.
  • Your consent record (this form's version and when you accepted it), your feedback, and basic usage logs needed to run the service.

How your voice may be used

  • Only for teaching and learning in LKCMedicine (NTU) courses and programmes — for example, narrating learning materials or answering students' questions in an LKCMedicine teaching chatbot.
  • Never for advertising or marketing, never for anything outside LKCMedicine teaching, and never sold or given to third parties for their own use.
  • Your voice is not used in a new course or material without the involvement of you or the course team responsible for it.
  • Anything spoken in your voice by these tools is generated by AI. LKCMedicine teaching tools identify AI-generated speech as such.

Who can access it

  • You can see, test and give feedback on your own voice only.
  • A small team of Voice Studio super users (LKCMedicine Digital Learning staff) can access recordings and voices to train them, check quality, and provide support and safety reviews.
  • Nobody else can select or use your voice through Voice Studio.

Where it is stored and for how long

  • In LKCMedicine-controlled cloud storage in Singapore, with the GPU that trains and runs voices in Seoul (South Korea).
  • We keep your recordings and voice model while they are used for LKCMedicine teaching, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Taking part is voluntary. Saying no has no effect on your role or your teaching.
  • You can withdraw at any time. Contact the LKCMedicine Digital Learning team and we will delete your recordings and voice models, and stop using your voice in new material.
  • Material already published with your voice is removed or replaced on request, as soon as reasonably possible.

Quality checks and limits

  • Every voice is checked by a super user before you are invited to test it. We may retrain it, or ask you to record again.
  • A synthetic voice is not perfect. It can mispronounce words or sound unnatural; you can correct pronunciations, and you can always ask us to stop using it.

Questions or requests (including withdrawal): contact the LKCMedicine Digital Learning team.
